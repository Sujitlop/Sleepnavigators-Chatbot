Heart Hospital of Austin Location and Arrival Instructions

Heart Hospital of Austin is located at 3801 N. Lamar Blvd., Austin, TX 78756. Phone: 512-407-7000. Patients should 
enter through the Emergency Room entrance and tell the ER registrar they are there for a sleep study. The ER 
registrar will call the sleep lab, and the sleep technician will escort the patient to the sleep lab.

WellNecessities Location and Parking Instructions

WellNecessities is located at 8835 Line Ave., Suite 500, Shreveport, LA 71106. Phone: 318-222-0885. Patients should
park in front of the building and enter the door directly in the middle of the building. The door says 
WellNecessities Sleep Lab by appointment only.

Methodist Main Location and Parking Instructions

Methodist Main is located at 4330 Medical Dr., Suite 140, San Antonio, TX 78229. Phone: 210-759-9100. The sleep lab 
is located across the street from the main hospital. Patients should park in front of the building. There is a 
drive-up portico where patients can be dropped off. Directly to the left of the portico is a glass door labeled 
Methodist Sleep Lab. Patients should ring the doorbell to the left of the door, and the sleep technician will come 
open the door.

Methodist Metropolitan Location and Parking Instructions

Methodist Metropolitan is located at 1303 E. McCullough, San Antonio, TX 78212. Phone: 210-575-2580. The sleep lab 
is in the Methodist Professional Building across the street from Methodist Metropolitan Hospital. Patients should 
park in one of the designated Sleep Lab Parking spots in front of the building. After parking, patients should call 
the phone number on the sign, and the sleep technician will come downstairs to open the building.
