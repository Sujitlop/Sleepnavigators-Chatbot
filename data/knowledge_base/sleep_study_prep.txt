What to Bring

Patients should bring something comfortable to sleep in, but no silk or satin pajamas. Patients should bring a list 
of medications. Patients may bring their own pillow or blanket if it makes them more comfortable.

Diabetic Patient Items

If diabetic, the patient should bring snacks and juice if needed. Bottled water will be provided at the sleep lab 
if needed.

Medication Instructions

All prescription medications should be taken as prescribed unless the patient takes prescription sleep aids. If the
do, they should bring those and talk with the technician about the appropriate time to take the medication.

Wake-Up Time

Wake-up time is between 5:00 a.m. and 5:30 a.m. The patient will be released to go home around 6:00 a.m.

Hair and Shower Instructions

Patients should shower before coming to the sleep study appointment. Hair should be freshly washed and dried with no 
styling products, oils, or chemicals in the hair. The sleep technician will need access to the scalp to put 
electrodes on the scalp.

Food, Eating, and Caffeine Instructions

Patients need to eat no less than 2 hours before the appointment. No caffeine after lunch. No meals should be brought
to the sleep lab. Only snacks for diabetic patients are allowed.

Nails

No artificial nails or dark nail polish colors.
