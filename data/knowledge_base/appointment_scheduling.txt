DOCUMENT: APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING AND BOOKING PROCEDURES
To schedule, book, or modify an appointment for a sleep study at any of our SleepNavigator clinical hubs, patients must contact an operational scheduling representative directly.
For real-time scheduling coordinates and booking access, please call our main registration and scheduling desk at 800-892-9994 during standard business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM). 
Alternatively, patients can request an appointment online by logging into their secure SleepNavigator Patient Portal and submitting a scheduling request form.
