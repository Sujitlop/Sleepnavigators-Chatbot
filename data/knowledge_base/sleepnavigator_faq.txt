What SleepNavigator Is

SleepNavigator is a workflow automation and patient navigation platform built specifically for managing sleep 
medicine patients. It streamlines the sleep study pipeline from referral intake to study completion, interpretation,
DME coordination, and compliance follow-up.

Who SleepNavigator Is Designed For

SleepNavigator is designed for independent sleep labs, hospital-based sleep centers, referring providers, sleep 
medicine physicians, DME providers, and administrative teams managing sleep study workflows.

Problems SleepNavigator Solves

SleepNavigator helps reduce duplicate data entry, delayed order processing, manual referral validation, report 
turnaround delays, DME follow-up gaps, and compliance tracking challenges.

Does SleepNavigator Replace an EMR

No. SleepNavigator works alongside the EMR. It is not intended to replace the primary EMR. It enhances sleep-
specific workflows that most EMRs do not handle efficiently.

Can SleepNavigator Integrate With Current Systems

Yes. SleepNavigator supports integration discussions with major EMRs and sleep scoring platforms. Depending on the 
system, it can import patient demographics and orders, export finalized reports, sync study statuses, and reduce 
duplicate documentation.

How AI Order Validation Works

SleepNavigator uses AI-powered validation to review uploaded referral orders, identify missing study types, 
diagnosis codes, patient demographics, and incomplete documentation, and reduce intake errors.

SleepNavigator HIPAA Compliance

SleepNavigator is designed with HIPAA compliance in mind, including secure data storage, role-based access controls, 
encrypted communication, and audit logging.

In-Lab and Home Sleep Testing Support

SleepNavigator supports in-lab PSG studies, split-night studies, titration studies, Home Sleep Testing, device 
tracking, and returns.

How SleepNavigator Reduces Turnaround Time

SleepNavigator helps reduce turnaround time by automating order validation, patient communication, study scheduling,
 status tracking, and report routing.

DME Coordination Support

SleepNavigator can track PAP setup, DME coordination, compliance follow-up, and patient communication. This helps 
reduce lost follow-ups and improve continuity of care.

SleepNavigator Implementation Timeline

Implementation timelines vary based on lab size, workflow complexity, and integration requirements. Typical 
onboarding is around 10 days.

SleepNavigator Support

SleepNavigator provides live onboarding sessions, workflow customization support, ongoing technical assistance, and 
direct contact with the team.

SleepNavigator Customization

SleepNavigator can tailor order validation rules, provider groups, facility configurations, reporting views, and 
user roles.

SleepNavigator Pricing

Pricing depends on lab volume, number of users, integration complexity, and feature needs. SleepNavigator offers 
scalable pricing models for smaller independent labs and multi-location systems.
